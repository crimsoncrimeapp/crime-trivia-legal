Privacy Policy

Crime Trivia (package com.crimetrivia.app) · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Crime Trivia mobile app ("the app") handles. The app is provided by ADLITQA S.R.L. ("we", "us").

In short: the app has no accounts and no ads. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device relates to the optional one-time purchase, and it is handled by Google Play and our purchase service, RevenueCat.

1. Data that stays on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your phone, using the app's local storage: your quiz progress and statistics, badges, streaks, remaining lives, sound and vibration settings, and an unfinished-round save. We never receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

2. Data collected when you make a purchase

The app offers an optional one-time purchase ("Full Clearance", unlimited lives). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm and restore your purchase we use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy), which acts as our service provider and receives:

  • an anonymous identifier generated for the app installation (not linked to your name or email);
  • purchase information (which product was bought, when, and the store transaction identifiers);
  • technical information sent automatically with any internet request, such as app version, device/OS type and IP address.

This data is used only to unlock and restore your purchase and to keep the purchase service working. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising.

3. Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising identifier, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Third parties

  • Google Play processes payments and licensing under Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat processes purchase data as described above.
  • The app contains optional links to book listings on Amazon. If you tap one, it opens in your browser and Amazon's privacy policy applies.

5. Retention and deletion

Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept for as long as needed to honour your purchase, unless you ask us to delete them. You can request deletion at any time by following the steps on our data deletion page. Records kept by Google Play (for example order and tax records) are governed by Google.

6. Children

The app describes real violent crimes and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/UK under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold only an anonymous purchase record, the quickest way to exercise these rights is the deletion request described above, or contacting us below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play data safety section.
