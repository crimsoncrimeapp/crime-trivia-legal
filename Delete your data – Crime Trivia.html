Delete your data

Crime Trivia (by ADLITQA S.R.L.) · Last updated: 21 October 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to utopiansmarketing@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Crime Trivia".
  2. Include the Google Play order number of your purchase (it looks like GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567 and is in your Google Play receipt email), or the Google account email used for the purchase, so we can find your record. If you never bought anything, we hold no personal data about you and there is nothing to delete.
  3. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The purchase record stored with our purchase service (RevenueCat): the anonymous app identifier and the purchase history linked to it.
After deletion we can no longer restore your purchase automatically. If you later reinstall the app, the premium unlock may need to be re-verified through Google Play.

What is not deleted by us

  • Payment, order and tax records kept by Google Play. They are controlled by Google; see Google's privacy policy.
  • Information we are legally required to keep, for the period the law requires.

Data stored only on your phone

Your progress, statistics, badges, lives and settings are stored locally on your device and never sent to us. To remove them, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → Crime Trivia → Storage → Clear data.

See also our Privacy Policy.
